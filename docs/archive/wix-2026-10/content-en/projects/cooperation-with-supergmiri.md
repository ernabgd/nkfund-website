# Collaboration with the Supergmiri platform

The Niko Kvaratskhelia Foundation has become a friend of the Supergmiri (“Superhero”) platform. As of today, the foundation of Niko, “the boy of the future”, has become a superhero for several beneficiaries 🦸.<br>
Supergmiri.ge is a transparent platform for connecting with beneficiaries, and “the boy of the future” motivates the beneficiaries to be determined to conquer the peaks of the future. Become our friend — and a friend of the platform, too.
