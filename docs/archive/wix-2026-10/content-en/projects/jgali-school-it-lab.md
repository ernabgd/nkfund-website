# IT lab at the public school in the village of Jgali

As part of the project, the school’s computer science room was refurbished and equipped with technology. Since 15 September, it has been named after Niko Kvaratskhelia.

“Wherever Niko’s name is heard, it means new challenges, new ideas, a better future — and all of this also means great responsibility. Proof of this is that today, with funding from Niko’s [foundation], an IT lab has opened at the Jgali school.” — Levan Kvaratskhelia, teacher at the public school in the village of Jgali
