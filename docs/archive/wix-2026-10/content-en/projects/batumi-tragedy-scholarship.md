# Scholarship for two children affected by the 2021 Batumi tragedy

“Tears and smiles of gratitude showed on people’s faces at the same time… then came applause, and once again talk of Niko’s light,” writes Tea Karchava, a teacher from Tsalenjikha, about the event dedicated to Terenti Graneli, at which the winning scholarship recipient was announced.
