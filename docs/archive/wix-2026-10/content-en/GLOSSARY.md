# Glossary: English renderings for nkfund.world

This file lists how each Georgian name and term on the site is written in English (translation of 2026-10-20). Use it when you add news or projects, so that names stay the same everywhere.

Items marked **Uncertain** need the foundation to confirm them; they are collected again at the end.

## 1. House style for the English site

- **Spelling:** British English (realise, organise, honour, programme).
- **Titles:** sentence case ("Our goal", "Funded projects"), as in the Georgian, which has no capital letters. Headlines have no final full stop.
- **Quotes:** curly quotes “…” and ’ in English; „…“ in Georgian. Put quotations upright, never in fake italics.
- **Dates:** "26 February 2023" and "September 2022" (en-GB). Georgian: „26 თებერვალი, 2023“ and „სექტემბერი, 2022“ (the browser's ka-GE format). Short form on cards: "26 Feb 2023".
- **The foundation:** first mention "the Niko Kvaratskhelia Foundation", after that "the foundation". „ნიკოს ფონდი“ → "Niko's foundation". The brand **nkfund.** (lower case, with the full stop) is never translated.
- **Names of people and places:** Georgian national romanisation (2002) without the apostrophes for ejective consonants, which is how Georgians write their names in English (კ, ქ → k; ტ, თ → t; პ, ფ → p; წ, ც → ts; ჭ, ჩ → ch; ხ → kh; ძ → dz; ჯ → j; ჟ → zh; შ → sh; ღ → gh; ყ → q).
- **"The regions" (რეგიონები):** in Georgian this means the country outside the capital. Keep "the regions" in quotations, and write "Georgia's regions" in our own sentences when the context alone is not clear.

## 8. Review log

**Translation review, 2026-10-20.** A separate AI reviewer agent, not the translator, checked the whole translation. This was not a native-speaker proof-read. It read the text sentence by sentence against the Georgian, looking for omissions, additions, changes in meaning, names, dates and tone. It also checked the new Georgian interface strings.

**What was checked:**
- All 13 Georgian Markdown files in `source/content/`, plus `site.yml` and `bank.yml`, against their English versions.
- Every `ka` / `en` pair in `ui.yml`.
- Every alt text in `media-alt.yml`, compared with the actual image.

**What was verified outside the text:**
- The SDG names in `ui.yml → sdg` match georgia.un.org/ka/sdgs. Goal 8 is printed there as „ეკომომიკური“; the fix to „ეკონომიკური“ is correct.
- The Batumi date, 8 October 2021, matches news reports of the building collapse and the date on the cover image. No background was added to the site text.
- The ka-GE and en-GB date formats in §1 and `ui.yml → dates` are what Node's `Intl.DateTimeFormat` produces.

After the fixes, all YAML still parses and the key-parity check still passes. `make_en.py` regenerates the Markdown files byte for byte. The same fixes were also made in its data file, `en_text.py`, so re-running the generator keeps them.
