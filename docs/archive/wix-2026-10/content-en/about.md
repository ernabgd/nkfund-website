# About us

The Niko Kvaratskhelia Foundation was founded in January 2022. It was set up by Niko’s family and friends.

Niko’s name is linked to many innovative projects. He created Feedc, a location-based platform, and with it he shattered a myth, proving that global projects can be created in a country as small as Georgia and can reach the whole world.<br>
Niko was a person of global vision, and his values were in line with the 17 Sustainable Development Goals that are now on the world’s agenda. Accordingly, Niko’s foundation will also serve the Sustainable Development Goals, so that young people can create a better and more sustainable future. The foundation’s aim is to follow Niko’s vision and to help young people realise their potential. To this end, the foundation will support innovative projects and education-focused initiatives.

The foundation decides by majority vote which projects to support and fund. Its board brings together successful people from various fields. Even complete strangers have come forward, offering to stand by the projects that Niko’s foundation will carry out.
